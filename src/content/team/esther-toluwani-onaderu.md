Esther Toluwani Onaderu is an executive and administrative professional with over four years of experience in strategic communications, digital media, stakeholder engagement, and project delivery. She is passionate about using effective communication and strategic planning to translate ideas into meaningful outcomes and advance initiatives with social impact.

At the Research Applied Institute for Sustainability in Science, Agriculture and Technology (RAISSAT), Esther serves as an Executive Assistant – Digital Communications & Research Content, supporting executive operations, coordinating activities, and developing digital content that communicates research, innovation, and sustainability to diverse audiences. Her work brings together organizational coordination, project support, content strategy, and research communication, helping connect institutional priorities with effective execution and public engagement.

Her professional interests span strategic storytelling, project management, digital communications, stakeholder relations, and sustainable development. She is particularly interested in the intersection of communication, collaboration, and project delivery in driving organizational effectiveness and meaningful social impact.
