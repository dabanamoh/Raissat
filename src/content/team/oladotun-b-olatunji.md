Oladotun Blessing Olatunji is a biotechnology researcher and Program Coordinator at the Research Applied Institute for Sustainability in Science, Agriculture and Technology (RAISSAT), where he coordinates multidisciplinary research projects, supports research teams, and contributes to scientific publications, technical reports, grant development, and research dissemination.

He holds an MSc in Biotechnology with Distinction from the University of Salford, UK, and is a recipient of the Global Gold Excellence Scholarship. His research interests span across industrial biotechnology, sustainable bioprocessing, and the circular bioeconomy, with a focus on converting agro-industrial waste into high-value, renewable resources. He contributes to multidisciplinary research, scientific publications, and research collaborations, and is a member of the International Network for Advancing Science and Policy (INASP).
