Shina James Owolabi, is a Programs Coordinator at the Research Applied institute for Sustainability in Science, Agriculture, and Technology (RAISSAT), where he coordinates multidisciplinary research projects and supports research teams, publications, and strategic collaborations.

He is currently pursuing a PhD in Animal Science (Agricultural Biochemistry and Nutrition) at the University of Ibadan, following an MSc in Animal Science from the same institution. He earned First-Class Honours in Agriculture (Animal Production) from the Federal University of Technology, Minna, where he was the overall best student in Animal Production and a recipient of the Federal Government Scholarship Award.

His research interests include precision animal nutrition, nutrigenomics, mathematical modelling, sustainable livestock production, and data-driven approaches to food security. He has contributed to peer-reviewed publications and collaborative research in animal nutrition, livestock production, and agricultural sustainability.
